# Natural Farming Glossary (Kannada–English)

A running glossary of Zero Budget Natural Farming (ZBNF) terms used across the episode documentation. Updated as new episodes are processed. Kannada terms are given in their commonly transliterated form; script is included on first entry.

| Term (Kannada script) | Transliteration | English meaning / definition | First introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| ಶೂನ್ಯ ಬಂಡವಾಳ ನೈಸರ್ಗಿಕ ಕೃಷಿ | Shoonya Bandavala Naisargika Krishi | Zero Budget Natural Farming (ZBNF) — farming method popularized by Subhash Palekar that avoids purchased chemical/external inputs, relying instead on soil microorganisms activated via cow dung/urine-based preparations. | EP1 |
| ಭೂಮಿ ತಾಯಿ, ಅನ್ನಪೂರ್ಣ | Bhoomi Tayi, Annapurna | "Mother Earth, Goddess of Nourishment" — the foundational metaphor that soil already contains everything a plant needs and nurtures all life without discrimination. | EP1 |
| ಜೀವಾಣುಗಳು | Jeevanugalu | Soil microorganisms; the biological agents that convert locked/unavailable soil nutrients into plant-available form. | EP1 |
| ಜೀವಾಮೃತ | Jeevamrutha | A fermented liquid culture (cow dung + cow urine + other ingredients) used to multiply beneficial soil microorganisms; applied to soil, not to leaves. | EP1 |
| ಬೀಜಾಮೃತ | Beejamrutha | A cow dung/urine-based seed treatment applied before sowing to protect and activate seeds. | EP1 |
| ಆಚ್ಛಾದನೆ | Aachadana | Mulching — covering soil to retain moisture and return organic matter; ZBNF recognizes multiple types (straw mulch, soil mulch, live/crop mulch). | EP1 |
| ವಾಫಸಾ | Waaphasa | Balance of air and moisture in the soil (soil aeration + moisture management), as opposed to waterlogging. | EP1 |
| ಅಕಡಿ ಸಾಲು | Akadi Salu | Traditional practice of alternating monocot (cereal) and dicot (legume) crop rows in intercropping/rotation. | EP1 |
| ಏಕದಳ | Ekadala | Monocot crops (e.g., ragi/finger millet, jowar/sorghum, paddy) — single-leaf/grain family. | EP1 |
| ದ್ವಿದಳ | Dwidala | Dicot crops (e.g., groundnut, cowpea, horse gram) — legume/pulse family. | EP1 |
| ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮ ತಂತು ಬೇರುಗಳು | Sookshma Tantu Beru / Root hairs | Fine capillary root hairs, the actual site of water/nutrient absorption in plants — typically located at the canopy drip-line, not the trunk base. | EP1 |
| ಕಳೆ | Kale | Weeds. In ZBNF philosophy, weeds are generally cut and left in place rather than removed from the field. | EP1 |
| ಸ್ವಯಂ ಅಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ | Swayam Abhivruddhi | Self-development — a seed's ability to grow into a full plant unaided, from seed to seed. | EP2 |
| ಸ್ವಯಂಪೋಷಿ | Swayamposhi | Self-nourishing — the forest's ability to feed itself without external input. | EP2 |
| ಬೆಳಕಿನ ಕೃಷಿ | Belakina Krishi | "Light farming" — designing crop placement and layering by each plant's sunlight requirement. | EP2 |
| ಜಲ ಕೃಷಿ | Jala Krishi | "Water farming" — using atmospheric moisture and groundwater recharge rather than only extraction. | EP2 |
| ಪೋಷಕಾಂಶ ಕೃಷಿ | Poshakamsha Krishi | "Nutrient farming" — the practice of unlocking existing soil nutrients via microbes rather than adding external fertilizer. | EP2 |
| ರೈಸೋಬಿಯಂ | Rhizobium | A nitrogen-fixing bacterium that lives in root nodules of leguminous (dicot) plants and converts atmospheric nitrogen into plant-usable nitrate. | EP2 |
| ಬೇರು ಗಂಟುಗಳು | Beru Gantugalu | Root nodules — small swellings on legume roots that house Rhizobium colonies; not to be confused with root-knot nematode disease. | EP2 |
| ರುಬಿಸ್ಕೋ ಪ್ರೋಟೀನ್ | RuBisCO Protein | The protein formed when raw sugar (from photosynthesis) combines with nitrogen; its accumulation is the plant's actual physical growth. | EP2 |
| ಬದುಮಣ್ಣು | Badu Mannu | "Bund soil" — soil taken from field edges/commons never exposed to chemicals, used as a microbial starter in Jeevamrutha. | EP2 |
| ಪರ್ಚ್ ಕ್ರಾಪ್ಸ್ | Perch Crops | Tall crops (e.g. jowar, maize) planted amid a field so birds perch on them and prey on pest insects — a natural pest control method. | EP2 |
| ಘನ ಜೀವಾಮೃತ | Ghana Jeevamrutha | Solid/dry form of Jeevamrutha (dung + jaggery + pulse flour + bund soil, dried and powdered) for use on rain-fed fields without irrigation. | EP3 |
| ಬುಟ್ಕಾಯಿ | Butkayi | Stunted, immature pods that result when the same legume crop is grown repeatedly in the same spot, causing root-nodule growth to stall. | EP3 |
| ಕೋಗುಲೇಷನ್ | Coagulation | The clumping together of soil particles into a more granular structure, improving water infiltration in heavy black (regur) soils. | EP3 |
| ಸೊಪ್ಪು ತುಳಿಯುವುದು | Soppu Tuliyuvudu | Traditional practice of treading nitrogen-rich green leaves (honge, neem, tangadi, ekke) into a paddy field before transplanting. | EP3 |
| ಗುರುತ್ವಾಕರ್ಷಣೆ | Gurutvakarshane | Gravity — the force that draws monsoon clouds across long distances and pulls fallen rain down into the soil. | EP4 |
| ಕೇಶಾಕರ್ಷಣೆ | Keshakarshane | Capillary action — the process by which water (and dissolved nutrients) already in the soil rises back up toward the surface as it warms and evaporates. | EP4 |
| ಕ್ಯಾನೋಪಿ | Canopy | The leaf canopy of a plant, described as its "face" — where stress, hunger, and excess water all visibly show up first. | EP4 |
| ಟ್ರಾನ್ಸ್‌ಪಿರೇಷನ್ | Transpiration | The process by which a plant releases the majority of absorbed water as vapor through its leaves to maintain internal balance. | EP4 |
| ರೆಸ್ಟಿಂಗ್ ಪೀರಿಯಡ್ | Resting Period | The dormant, leaf-shedding period after harvest (autumn) during which a plant's remaining roots decompose, loosening the soil for the next season. | EP4 |
| ನಾಟಿ/ಜವಾರಿ ಬೀಜಗಳು | Naati/Jawari Beejagalu | Native/landrace seeds — traditional, non-hybrid seed varieties that grow larger plants with deeper roots, making them more drought-resilient. | EP4 |
| ತಾಯಿ ಬೇರು | Taayi Beru | Taproot — the deep primary root that draws moisture and nutrients from deep soil during drought stress, when surface moisture is unavailable. | EP4 |
| ಆಚ್ಛಾದನೆ/ಒದಿಕೆ | Aachadane/Odike | Mulching — the third of ZBNF's four wheels, comprising three sub-types: soil mulching (tillage/weeding), live mulching (companion cropping), and dry mulching (straw cover). | EP5 |
| ಮಾಗಿ ಉಳುಮೆ | Maagi Ulume | Pre-monsoon deep tillage done to draw nutrients from decomposed crop residue into the soil ahead of the first rains. | EP5 |
| ಬಹುಮಡಿ | Bahumadi | The multi-tier/multi-storey planting system (big tree → small tree → shrub → bush → vine/grass) used mainly in horticulture and orchards. | EP5 |
| ತವರು ಮನೆ | Tavaru Mane | "Trap crop" — a deliberately planted sacrificial crop (e.g. castor, arali) that draws pest insects away from the main crop; must not be sprayed. | EP5 |
| ನಡ ಮುರಿಯುವುದು | Nada Muriyuvudu | The practice of gently treading and breaking the central shoot of a young ragi/aarka plant to stimulate tillering (more side shoots) about 20 days later. | EP5 |
| ಹ್ಯೂಮಸ್ | Humus | "Jeevana dravya" — organic matter formed from carbon and nitrogen combining in soil; its accumulation improves soil's physical, chemical, and biological properties. | EP5 |
| ಸಾವಯವ ಇಂಗಾಲ | Savayava Ingala | Organic carbon — a key soil fertility index; ideal is 2.5-3%, most farmland today sits near 0.2%, ZBNF plots can reach 3.5-4%. | EP6 |
| C:N ಅನುಪಾತ | C:N Anupata | Carbon-to-Nitrogen ratio (~10:1) required for organic carbon to permanently bind with nitrogen and form humus, rather than escaping back as CO2. | EP6 |
| ಗ್ಲಿರಿಸಿಡಿಯಾ | Glyricidia (Seemangi / Gobbarada Soppu) | A soft-wooded, nitrogen-fixing tree planted among orchard rows to supply nitrogen to young horticultural saplings. | EP6 |
| ಅಗಸೆ | Agase (Chogache / Sede — Sesbania) | A fast-growing, soft-stemmed nitrogen-fixing tree used similarly to Glyricidia in orchard intercropping design. | EP6 |
| ಬೇರು ಕಾಲು | Beru Kaalu | A widely-held misconception that plants suffer from roots physically "stepping on" each other; the real cause is competition for scarce nutrients, especially nitrogen. | EP7 |
| ಕೋನಿಂಗ್ | Coning | Staggered-timing planting — planting fast, tall "giant trees" first and adding companion trees around them only after they've gained height, to avoid shade competition. | EP7 |
| ಚೆಂಡು ಹೂವು | Chenduhoovu (Marigold) | Marigold — the only known natural remedy for root-knot nematode disease; its roots secrete Alpha-Terthienyl, which kills the pathogen; also attracts pollinators and can be sold. | EP7 |
| ಆಲ್ಫಾ ಟೆಟ್ರಾಥಯಿನೈಲ್ | Alpha-Terthienyl | The nematode-killing chemical compound secreted by marigold roots. | EP7 |
| ವೆಲ್ವೆಟ್ ಬೀನ್ಸ್ | Velvet Beans (Nasugunni) | A vigorous, sprawling legume used as ground cover between young orchard trees to suppress weeds and fix nitrogen while awaiting the main crop's maturity. | EP7 |
| ಆಂಟಿ-ಜರ್ಮಿನೇಟಿಂಗ್ ಪ್ರಾಪರ್ಟೀಸ್ | Anti-Germinating Properties | A seed's built-in dormancy/protection mechanism that prevents every fallen seed from sprouting (natural population control); Beejamrutha treatment safely helps seeds overcome this, raising germination from 60-80% to over 95%. | EP7 |
| ಇಳೆ ಬಿಡುವುದು | Ile Biduvudu | The step in Beejamrutha preparation where a cloth bundle of dung is suspended in water overnight to release its microorganisms. | EP8 |
| ಭಕ್ಷಕ ಜೀವಾಣುಗಳು | Bhakshaka Jeevanugalu | "Predator microorganisms" that emerge once a Jeevamrutha drum's microbial population outgrows its space, beginning to destroy the excess — the reason liquid Jeevamrutha must be used within 7-10 days. | EP8 |
| ಪಂಚಮಹಾಭೂತಗಳು | Panchamahabhutagalu | The five classical elements (earth, water, fire, air, space) whose approximate proportions in plant/animal bodies are used to explain why only ~1.5% of a plant's mass ("earth element") comes from soil. | EP8 |
| ವಾಫಸಾ | Vaphasa | ZBNF's fourth and final wheel — the balanced state of soil pores holding roughly equal air and water, achieved via Jeevamrutha microbes and intercrop root decomposition. | EP8 |
| ನೀಮಾಸ್ತ್ರ | Neemastra | A neem-leaf-based botanical spray for sap-sucking pests (aphids, mealybugs); made with neem leaf, cow urine, and fresh dung, steeped 24-48 hours; must be used within 1-2 days. | EP9 |
| ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಾಸ್ತ್ರ | Brahmastra | A multi-leaf botanical spray (neem plus several other leaves) for fruit and pod borers; steeped 48 hours in cow urine; can be stored up to 6 months. | EP9 |
| ಅಗ್ನಿಅಸ್ತ್ರ | Agniastra | A stronger tobacco-and-hot-chili-based preparation for stem borers, boiled and applied by spot-spraying or syringe injection directly into the borer's hole. | EP9 |
| ಸಾಂಬ್ರಾಣಿ-ಗಂಜಲ | Sambrani-Gomutra | A simple sap-sucking pest remedy made from benzoin resin (sambrani) steeped in cow urine for 7-8 days. | EP9 |
| ನೀರ್ ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ | Neer Majjige | A fermented sour-buttermilk fungicide made from native cow's milk, curdled, churned, diluted, and soured for 2 days; safe enough to drink; stores up to 6 months. | EP9 |
| ಬಸಿ ಕಾಲುವೆ | Basi Kaluve | Drainage channels dug between trees in waterlogged or clay-heavy land to prevent fungal disease caused by standing water. | EP9 |
| ಜೈವಿಕ ಬಲೆ | Jaivika Bale (Bio-catch) | Natural pest suppression that emerges when many intercrop species attract a mix of parasitic and predator insects, which then keep pest populations in check without any spraying. | EP9 |
| ದಶಪರ್ಣಿ | Dashaparni | A potent botanical concoction made from ten kinds of leaves plus dung and cow urine, fermented 15-30 days; used against stubborn pests that don't respond to Neemastra/Brahmastra/Agniastra. | EP10 |
| ಗಮೋಸಿಸ್ | Gummosis (Kanda Soruvike) | Fungal-driven sap oozing from wounds or nicks in tree trunks/stems, common in coconut; triggered by physical injury combined with excess moisture or heat. | EP10 |
| ಪನಾಮ ವಿಲ್ಟ್ | Panama Wilt | A banana disease causing leaves to dry from the tip and yellow/brown before dropping, rooted in an "it needs lots of water" mindset that leads to overwatering. | EP10 |
| ನಾಚಿಕೆ ಗಿಡ | Nachike Gida (Touch-me-not, Mimosa pudica) | A thorny, leaf-folding plant used in a botanical spray against thrips/mite damage; also treated as a beneficial weed to be cut only after flowering but before seeding. | EP10 |
| ಹಿಂಗಾರಿ/ಮುಂಗಾರಿ | Hingari/Mungari | The post-monsoon (rabi) and monsoon (kharif) growing seasons; sowing a crop in the wrong season strips it of the natural disease resistance it would otherwise have. | EP10 |
| ದ್ವಿತೀಯ ಸಂಶ್ಲೇಷಣೆ | Photosynthesis (Dwiteeya Samshleshane) | The leaf process converting CO2 + water + sunlight into raw sugar (C6H12O6), releasing oxygen and surplus water back to the atmosphere. | EP11 |
| ಪತ್ರಹರಿತ್ತು | Chlorophyll (Patraharittu) | The pigment that makes leaves green and drives photosynthesis. | EP11 |
| ಬೇರು ಸೂಸುವಿಕೆ | Root Exudation (Beru Soosuvike) | The process by which a plant releases part of its manufactured food around its roots to feed soil microbes, which in turn convert unavailable soil nutrients into a plant-usable form. | EP11 |
| ಬೇಸಿನ್ ಮೆಥಡ್ | Basin Method | Traditional irrigation practice of building a raised basin around a tree trunk and pooling water there; becomes ineffective once the canopy outgrows the basin. | EP12 |
| ಚಾಮರ / ಕ್ಯಾನೋಪಿ | Chamara/Canopy | The full spread of a plant's leaf canopy; the root system generally spreads to roughly the same width, which is where water and nutrients should be applied. | EP12 |
| ಉತ್ತರಾಯಣ/ದಕ್ಷಿಣಾಯನ | Uttarayana/Dakshinayana | The sun's apparent northward movement (Dec 21-Jun 20) and southward movement (Jun 21-Dec 20); used to decide the correct north-south row orientation for crops. | EP12 |
| ದೈತ್ಯ ಮರ / ಧಾನ್ಯ | Daithya Mara / Dhanya | A basic crop-planning classification: large horticultural "giant trees" (mango, cashew, tamarind) versus sun-hungry grain crops (rice, ragi, wheat, jowar). | EP12 |
| ಪಂಚತರಂಗಿಣಿ ಯೋಜನೆ | Panchatarangini (Five-Tier System) | A five-layer crop-planning framework: giant tree, small tree, shrub, small plant/vine, and tuber-plus-litter — combined for maximum yield per plot. | EP13 |
| ಫುಟ್ ಕ್ಯಾಂಡಲ್ | Foot Candle | A unit of light intensity (light from a candle at one foot); giant trees and grain crops need over 7,000 foot-candles of peak summer sun to fruit well. | EP13 |
| ಏಕ ಕಣ್ಣಿನ ಬೀಜ ಪದ್ಧತಿ | Single-Eye-Sett Method | A sugarcane seed-saving technique: each bud/eye is cut with an uneven ratio (1/3 base, 2/3 tip) and Beejamrutha-treated individually, cutting seed use from ~4,000 kg/acre to 250-400 kg/acre. | EP13 |
| ಕುಟುಂಬ ಸಾಲು | Kutumba Salu (Family Row) | Within the sugarcane furrow system, the row reserved specifically for household food grains (rice, ragi, wheat) rather than cash-crop vegetables. | EP14 |
| ಕೂಳೆ ಬೆಳೆ | Koole Bele (Ratoon Crop) | The second flush of cane that regrows from the stubble/roots after the first harvest, without replanting. | EP14 |
| ರೌದಿ | Roudi | The leftover cane leaves and trash after harvest, spread as mulch on the neighbouring row rather than burned. | EP14 |
| ಅಗಸೆ (ಮರ) | Agase Tree (Sesbania grandiflora) | A perennial nitrogen-fixing tree used in banana orchards alongside moringa and glyricidia to keep supplying nitrogen after legume intercrops get shaded out. | EP15 |
| ಡ್ಯಾನ್ಸಿಂಗ್ ಲೈಟ್ಸ್ ಪರಿಣಾಮ | Dancing Lights Effect | The dappled sunlight created as breeze moves the leaves of tall nitrogen-fixing trees, letting filtered light and partial shade reach the crops below. | EP15 |
| ಹುಳಿ ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ | Huli Majjige (Sour Buttermilk Fungicide) | The full recipe for a key fungicide: milk is cultured into curd, churned to buttermilk without butter forming, then fermented 2-3 days until sharply sour before diluting for spray; used against Panama Wilt, mildew, and other fungal issues. | EP16 |
| ಬಂಚಿ ಟಾಪ್ | Bunchy Top | A viral banana disease where leaves suddenly shrink into broom-like clusters; the guest advises against the common practice of burning infected plants, favoring mulch and extra Jeevamrutha instead. | EP16 |
| ಚಿಲ್ಕ ನೀರು | Chilka Neeru | The very low-moisture state a ripe coconut must be dried down to (only 2-3 spoons of water left inside) before it's suitable for sowing as seed. | EP19 |
| ಮಡಿ | Madi | A nursery bed used for sprouting seeds, nuts, or paddy seedlings before transplanting. | EP19 |
| ಎಫ್‌ವೈಎಂ | FYM (Farm Yard Manure) | Plain dried, powdered, sieved native-cow dung stored on its own (without the fermented Jeevamrutha additions), used as a simpler soil input. | EP23 |
| ಮೆಟಾರೈಜಿಯಂ | Metarhizium | A beneficial fungus present in solid Jeevamrutha; white grubs and dung beetles that feed on the Jeevamrutha ingest it and are killed from within, giving natural grub control without a separate pesticide. | EP24 |
| ಅಕ್ಕಡಿ ಸಾಲು | Akkadi Salu (Strip Intercrop) | A block of several rows (e.g. 3-4) set aside within a tall grain crop like jowar for a shorter companion crop (safflower, linseed, chana), making the shorter crop easier to manage than if scattered through every row. | EP24 |
| ಪಗಡೆ ದಾಯ | Pagade Daya (Checkerboard Spacing) | A grid-pattern single-seedling wide spacing (8, 10, 12, or 16 inches square) for direct-sown or transplanted rice, allowing each plant's roots to spread freely on all sides. | EP25 |
| ಗುತ್ತಿ ನಾಟಿ | Gutthi Naati (Clump Transplanting) | The conventional practice of transplanting 4-5 rice seedlings together per spot out of fear of poor germination; blocks airflow and sunlight, causing fungal blast disease and lower yield. | EP25 |
| ಅರಿ ಬಿಡುವುದು | Ari Biduvudu (Windrow Drying) | Leaving cut grain stalks lying in the row for a few days after harvest so remaining nutrients in the still-green leaf/stem transfer into the grain, reducing chaff/empty grains. | EP26 |
| ಬರಬತ್ತ | Barabhatta (Dry-Sown Rice) | Direct dry-cultivation of rice without a flooded nursery: seed is soaked 6 hours, treated in Beejamrutha, and sown 1-2 seeds per spot in a checkerboard grid with solid Jeevamrutha. | EP26 |
| ಸಹಜೀವನ ಗಿಡಗಳು | Sahajeevana Gidagalu (Symbiotic Plants) | Companion plants grown together in a polyculture whose roots interlock underground and exchange nutrients, while also disrupting pest life cycles compared to monocropping. | EP27 |
| ಜೀವನದ್ರವ್ಯ / ಹ್ಯೂಮಸ್ | Jeevanadravya / Humus | The dark organic substance formed when 10 carbon particles bond with 1 nitrogen particle as dead organisms and plant waste decompose; 1kg of it absorbs about 6L of atmospheric moisture per day, explaining why some natural-farming fields look dry on top yet stay green. | EP28 |
| ಸಸಿ ಗಡಸುಗೊಳಿಸುವಿಕೆ | Hardening of Seedlings | Acclimatizing shade-raised nursery seedlings to open sunlight for 2-3 days before transplanting, to prevent transplant shock and wilting. | EP29 |
| ತಂತಿ ಪೈರು / ದಾಯಿದ ಪೈರು | Tanti Pairu / Dayida Pairu | A traditional row-spacing pattern for direct-sown ragi/millet where an extra 1-foot row is inserted between the main 2x2ft grid lines to increase plant density without crowding. | EP36 |
| ಹುಳಿ ಮಜ್ಜಿಗೆ | Hulimajjige (Sour Buttermilk) | A fungicide made by fermenting butter-skimmed buttermilk (diluted 1:4 with water) for 2-5 days until sharply sour, used against yellowing, blast, and leaf-curl diseases. | EP16, EP37 |
| ದಶಪರ್ಣಿ | Dashaparni (Ten-Leaf Kashaya) | The strongest-tier botanical pesticide, made from ten kinds of leaves (neem, papaya, custard apple, etc.) plus tobacco, chili, garlic, turmeric and ginger, fermented in shade for 40 days; used when Agniastra/Brahmastra fail to control severe pest outbreaks. | EP9-10, Ref.
